# 0007 · No stealth features

**Context.** Tools in this category are often marketed for hiding AI help during interviews or calls (overlays invisible to screen sharing, "undetectable" modes). Pinganillo processes other people's voices, and recording and transcription laws differ by country; in some places every participant must consent.

**Decision.** Pinganillo will not add features whose purpose is to hide its use. It ships with a first-run notice, a one-click meeting notice, a clear local/cloud indicator per provider, and audio that never leaves the PC.

**Consequences.** Some users will want a stealth mode and will not find it here; forks are possible under the MIT license, but the project's defaults and identity stay transparent. See [responsible use](../responsible-use.md).
